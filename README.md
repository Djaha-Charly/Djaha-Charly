## Featured projects — findings before tools

### 01 · French billion-euro companies | Financial performance

**Business question:** Which high-revenue companies demonstrate strong profitability, and which deserve closer financial attention?

Using French **INPI/BCE financial data**, enriched with **SIRENE**, I developed an analytical workflow and Power BI dashboards covering **2016–2024**, focused on companies in the billion-euro revenue segment.

**What the analysis shows:**
- Revenue rankings alone cannot describe financial health: the dashboards compare **revenue, net margins and growth**.
- Companies with **negative net margins** and those with **revenue growth above 10%** can be identified for deeper investigation.
- Financial performance can be explored by year, sector and revenue-based company segments.

**Business value:** Distinguish business size from profitability and growth to support more informed financial benchmarking.

**Tools:** SQL · Python · DuckDB · dbt · Power BI

### 02 · European pharmacy sales | Revenue vs. margin

**Business question:** Which products and markets generate the most sales — and which deliver stronger margins?

I analysed **synthetic retail data** representing **120 pharmacies across eight European countries**. The figures below are from the **2025 dashboard view**.

**Key findings from the simulation:**
- **€4.41M in revenue**, **€1.24M in total margin** and a **28.11% overall margin rate**.
- **Prescription products** generated approximately **€1.44M in revenue**, at a **22% margin rate**, compared with **33.7% for Wellness** and **33.5% for Personal Care**.
- Promotional transactions accounted for **10.3% of revenue** with a **20% margin rate**, below the overall average. This comparison does **not** establish the causal impact of promotions.

**Business value:** Reveal gaps between sales volume and profitability, informing category-level reporting and promotional review.

**Tools:** Python · SQL · DuckDB · dbt · Power BI

[Explore the project](https://github.com/Djaha-Charly/european-pharmacy-bi-analytics) · [View dashboard](https://github.com/Djaha-Charly/european-pharmacy-bi-analytics/blob/main/screenshots/Executive_Overview.png) · [Synthetic dataset (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ehmadali/eu-pharmacy-products-and-pricing)

### 03 · E-commerce customer behavior | Segmentation & retention

**Business question:** Which customers create the most value, and who should be prioritised for retention or re-engagement?

I examined a **simulated dataset of 350 customers** and built a Power BI report linking purchase behaviour, membership, satisfaction and inactivity.

**Key findings from the simulation:**
- **Gold customers spent about $1,311 on average** versus **$473 for Bronze customers** — approximately **2.77× more**.
- Spending and the number of items purchased had a **0.97 correlation** within this sample (association, not causation).
- Segmentation identified **131 loyal, active customers**, **55 customers to re-engage** and **11 at-risk customers**.

**Business value:** Identify valuable customer segments, prioritise outreach and guide retention initiatives using data.

**Tools:** Python · pandas · Matplotlib · Seaborn · Power BI

[Explore the project](https://github.com/Djaha-Charly/Ecommerce_Customer_Behavior_Analysis) · [View segmentation dashboard](https://github.com/Djaha-Charly/Ecommerce_Customer_Behavior_Analysis/blob/main/image/Segmentation%20et%20Fid%C3%A9lisation.png) · [Dataset source (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/thedevastator/customer-behaviour)
